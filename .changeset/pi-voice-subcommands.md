Replace the `/voice-*` commands with one `/voice` command and subcommands: `/voice` (record), `/voice cancel`, `/voice device`, `/voice profile`, `/voice profile add`, `/voice profile model`, `/voice doctor` and `/voice status`. Subcommands autocomplete after `/voice `. The old `/voice-record`, `/voice-cancel`, `/voice-device`, `/voice-profile`, `/voice-doctor` and `/voice-status` commands are removed.
